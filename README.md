# Historical Manuscripts on the Kung–Traub Conjecture

**Rodrigo Alberto Castro Marín**  
*Research manuscripts, 17–18 September 2026*

I had been working on the Kung–Traub conjecture for several years. A decisive stage of this research culminated on 17 September 2026 and in the early hours of 18 September 2026, when successive manuscripts presenting a proposed proof of the order bound were completed. **All the historical manuscripts were originally written in Spanish, my native language**, and are preserved here in their original form.

During this stage, I discussed my ideas, partial results, and unresolved mathematical questions with ChatGPT. The dialogue helped me work through difficulties involving the formalization of memoryless iterative methods, the zeros of auxiliary polynomials and their Wronskians, and quantitative estimates for perturbations preserving previously acquired function data.

The 44-page manuscript compiled on **17 September 2026 at 23:17 (Chile local time)** contains an entire-function construction and a proposed proof of the order barrier. The 57-page manuscript compiled on **18 September 2026 at 02:22 (Chile local time)** further develops the operational interpretation of evaluation cost, exact representation of procedures, and a corollary addressing the Kung–Traub bound.
